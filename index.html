About

I’m Younus, a business student at Bellevue College and an incoming Informatics and Business Administration double major. I’m interested in the space between people, technology, and the decisions that make a business work.

My projects began with businesses I could understand firsthand: restaurant sales, ingredient costs, customer demand, and daily operations. I’ve built SQL database designs, dashboards, and profitability models to make those relationships easier to explore.

I also bring a hands-on perspective. I’ve led mechanical development for a FIRST Tech Challenge team, tested user flows at PathSpark, and worked with students at the YMCA. I like getting close to a problem, understanding the people involved, and building from there.
